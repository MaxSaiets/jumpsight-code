      ISU TERM

      Under-rotation

      How the ISU technical panel marks rotation: “q” — landed about a quarter short (≈90°), full base value, GOE reduced. “<” — short by more than 90° and less than 180°, reduced base value. “<<” — 180° or more, scored as the jump with one revolution less.

      Most common causes: a loose or late air position, or too little height and air time (causes base, strong evidence).
