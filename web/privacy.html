Privacy

Last updated: October 9, 2026 · This is an early test version of the app.

What JumpSight does

You upload a phone video of a figure skating jump. The app measures the jump (name, rotation count, air time, height, visible execution errors) and builds a training session from it.

What we store

  • Your video and the body-position data computed from it (joint coordinates frame by frame).
  • Analysis results: jump name, counts, measurements and errors, kept in your attempt journal.
  • Profile: the name you enter, your role (skater or coach), answers to the optional “About you” questions and fitness tests, completed sessions.
  • Sign-in: if you create an account — your email and a salted password hash; with Google sign-in — your Google account id, email and profile picture.
  • Coach links: which coach you accepted, comments and focus they set.
  • Your answers to “Did the system get the jump right?”.

How long

  • Uploaded videos and their body-position data are deleted automatically 72 hours after the analysis.
  • If you agree that a video may be used to improve the system, that video and its body-position data are kept until you withdraw the agreement. Nothing is pre-selected; you can withdraw on the analysis screen.
  • Your journal, profile and coach links are kept while you use the app, or until you ask us to delete them.

Who processes data for us

  • Modal — hosting and video processing.
  • Supabase — storage of sign-in sessions.
  • OpenAI — when you ask the coach chat a question that has no built-in answer, the question text (and, for questions about your own progress, a short summary of your profile and latest analysis) is sent to generate an answer. Videos are never sent.
  • Google — only if you choose Google sign-in.

We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Coaches

A coach sees your analyses only after you accept their request. You can remove a coach at any time in Profile.

Children

If you are under 13, a parent or guardian must set up and use the app with you.

Delete your data or ask a question

Write to {{CONTACT}} and we will delete your journal, profile and any videos you agreed to keep, usually within 7 days.

Questions about how measurements are made are answered in the app under Training → Ask the coach.
